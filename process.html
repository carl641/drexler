Our process

Glass is our expertise, so it doesn’t have to be yours.

We walk you through every step so you get a product that catches the eye, improves the flow of your space and works beautifully. From construction tips early on to cleaning advice years later, we’re always happy to talk.

01

Send rough dimensions

For shower doors and enclosures, all we need to start is the rough width and height of your shower space. Add a photo for more complex layouts, plus the plumbing finish you’d like us to match. For mirrors or tabletops, rough measurements are enough to price the job.

02

Receive options & pricing

We’ll come back with options and a price, usually within one business day, and schedule an on-site meeting.

03

On-site evaluation

We take exact measurements, review hardware options and answer your questions. We also look at the flow of the room to make sure you’ve chosen the right product for the space.

04

Fabricate & install

Your glass is cut and fabricated in our Atlanta workshop, then installed by a two-person Drexler field team that protects your tile, stone and home along the way.

Planning other renovations? Let us know. Our experience often lets us spot design issues early or suggest options you might not have considered. You’re welcome to visit the showroom at any stage. Sometimes it helps to get your hands on the product.

Drexler FAQ

We have answers.

Customers have asked us a lot of questions over 70 years. Here are the ones we hear most.

Ask us anything
Who installs barn doors for showers?
We do. Our exposed-roller barn door and bypass enclosures are custom-fitted and installed by our own field teams, in brushed brass, polished or brushed stainless, matte black and brushed bronze. Most need no bottom track.
Where can I see choices for shower doors?
At our showroom at 1452 Northside Drive, open Monday through Friday from 7:30 am to 4:15 pm. You’ll find full-size frameless and sliding displays, hardware, and glass in a variety of colors and patterns.
Can Drexler help with shower door hardware?
Yes. We carry Atlanta’s largest selection of shower door hardware: hinges, knobs, pulls, towel bars and robe hooks in finishes that complement your fixtures. Our designers will help you choose.
What is low-iron glass?
Standard clear glass contains iron oxide, which adds a green tint you can see on the edges of thick panels. Low-iron glass, such as Starphire®, has much less iron, so it’s clearer and shows your tile and stone in true color.
What is the best glass protection for shower doors?
We recommend VectraShield, a permanent coating applied at the factory that repels water, oil, soap scum and minerals. It never needs reapplying and carries a lifetime warranty against stains, corrosion and hard-water buildup.
Does Drexler sell antique mirrors?
Yes. We stock factory antique mirror and distress our own in-house. We can match a mirror you already own, cut diamond tiles, apply panels to furniture or create a one-of-a-kind finish.
Does Drexler offer restaurant sneeze guards?
We do. Our custom frameless glass sneeze guards are sturdier and better looking than off-the-shelf systems, and unlike acrylic they won’t cloud with repeated sanitizing.
Can frameless doors be installed over a tub?
Absolutely. Tub/shower combinations are among our most common configurations, as fixed screens, hinged doors or sliding bypass systems.
How do I maintain a frameless glass door?
Squeegee after use and clean with a standard, non-abrasive glass cleaner. Adding VectraShield protection makes upkeep much easier. Ask us for tips specific to your glass.
