How we work

Trust comes first.

We’re in your home, in the rooms you use every day. If we make a mistake, we tell you and we fix it. Builders and architects keep calling us back because once they hand off a job, they don’t have to worry about it.

Our own crews

Every job is measured on site and installed by two-person Drexler field teams, never subcontractors. They’re trained in manners and care as much as in glazing.

Made in Atlanta

We cut, polish and fabricate in our Northside Drive workshop. Measure twice, cut once. Nothing comes off a big-box shelf.

Straight talk

We’re upfront about timelines, prices and what a product can and can’t do. No jargon, no promises we can’t keep.
