02 · Glass upgrade

Ultra-clear low-iron glass

Standard clear glass contains iron, which gives it a green tint, especially along the edges of heavy frameless panels. Low-iron glass, such as Starphire®, removes most of that tint. You see your tile, marble and fixtures in their true color, not the glass.

  • Ideal with white tile, marble and large-format porcelain
  • Brighter, more open feel with less green at the edges
  • Available for doors, fixed panels, steam showers and barn doors

Compare standard and low-iron glass side by side in our showroom.

03 · Glass care

VectraShield glass protection

Under a microscope, shower glass is pitted and porous. Hard water and soap slowly etch it until it clouds. VectraShield is a permanent coating applied at the factory that becomes part of the glass, so it stays clear for good.

  • Lifetime warranty against stains, corrosion and hard-water buildup
  • Repels water and oil, so you clean less
  • No reapplication, revitalizers or harsh cleaners
  • Can be applied to both sides of the glass
04 · Sliding systems

Barn door & bypass enclosures

These aren’t your grandmother’s sliding doors. Our exposed-roller systems use a minimal, European-inspired design: clean glass panels hung on precision stainless steel rollers, with no bulky frame. They glide side to side, so they’re ideal where a swinging door won’t fit.

The solid-brass Heritage Classic Series brings back the quality of the 1940s and ’50s. Every hinge and screw is solid brass.
