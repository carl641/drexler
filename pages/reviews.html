Featured projects

Some of our work.

From revitalizing Atlanta neighborhoods to partnering with award-winning designers, every project is our favorite. Here are a few from recent years.

ABODE · Mattie Branch

Low-iron glass and matte black, south of DeKalb Avenue.

We love working on architect Christopher Leerssen’s boutique projects, which are revitalizing neighborhoods around Reynoldstown. At Mattie Branch, some all-white baths got low-iron Starphire® glass that almost disappears. Others got matte black barn door systems that nod to the neighborhood’s industrial roots.

Atlanta Homes & Lifestyles · Bath Contest Winner

Glass bent to follow every curve.

For designer Gunter Sprang, we used ½" low-iron ShowerGuard tempered glass so the split-face travertine kept its true color, and bent it to echo the room’s undulating ceiling and inset shelf. On Joe Drexler’s suggestion, a flat-screen TV was hidden behind a transparent vanity mirror.

Keiffer Phillips · Patricia Brown Builders

A mid-century peaceful retreat.

To keep the travertine running uninterrupted, David Drexler suggested clear floor-to-ceiling glass with as little hardware as possible. The water-closet etching was set 6" from the wall and 12" off the floor so the view of the tile continues through.
