Restaurants

Set the tone, with an eye for safety.

For decades, some of Atlanta’s favorite restaurants have trusted us with their glass. Our frameless sneeze guards look and feel more solid than off-the-shelf systems, so they become a feature rather than an afterthought. Unlike acrylic, glass won’t cloud under constant sanitizing.

  • Custom sneeze guards and protective barriers
  • Mirrors, bar tops, tabletops, cabinets and shelving
  • Fabricated in our Atlanta shop to your exact shape and color
Multifamily

Condos & apartments that feel like home.

We bring the detail we put into Atlanta’s finest custom homes to multifamily projects: shower and spa enclosures, mirrors, color-glass walls, balustrades and railings. For building managers and maintenance teams, we can replace fogged windows, patio door glass and shower doors.

Retail & office
