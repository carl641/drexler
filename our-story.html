Our story

To beautify built environments.

Glass became a serious building material at the Great Exhibition of 1851, when Joseph Paxton’s Crystal Palace showed what sheet glass could do. Glass still inspires us with how it shapes light and temperature, and how it reflects and magnifies. We believe our work adds to the enjoyment of every space we help create.

Since 1953

In 1953, Cecil J. Drexler designed a premium line of aluminum shower and tub enclosures that set a new quality standard for the industry.

Sold through Sears at first, they were heavier and sturdier than anything else on the market, with larger rollers and reinforced hinges, at affordable prices. Word spread about these finely crafted, virtually “indestructible” doors, and demand grew.

Over the following decades Cecil added product lines and built a reputation for excellent service. Drexler became the go-to glass supplier for many of Atlanta’s most prestigious builders and architects.

In the early ’80s, as framed doors fell out of favor, Cecil’s son Joe began experimenting with a frameless shower technology from Europe that few people knew about. In 1984 we installed our first frameless shower door. Today our shower doors are in the smallest bungalows and the largest estates and hotels.

Milestones

Seven decades, one family.

We’re still family-owned. Our long relationships with remodelers, architects and designers have led to award-winning glass projects across the Southeast.

Field teams

We treat your home like our own.

Every field team is two highly trained professionals, and their training goes well beyond glass and glazing. We spend real time on values, manners and kindness.
